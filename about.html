About The Beacon Centre

A steady light for when things feel unclear.

The Beacon Centre is a therapy and counselling practice built around three ideas: healing, clarity and wholeness. We offer structured, professional support for individuals, couples, groups, leaders and organisations.

Meet your therapist

Michelle Tobi-Adesanya

Michelle is an emotional healing and growth specialist, professional counsellor and CBT-trained therapist, whose work is dedicated to helping people find healing, purpose and wholeness. She trained as a professional counsellor at ADI.

What sets her apart is the way she brings faith-based principles together with evidence-based strategies, creating a safe space where people feel seen, heard and empowered. In her own words, she combines empathy with practical professional tools, so you can find clarity, heal deeply and grow into the best version of yourself.

Her work spans children, teenagers and adults, walking with them through seasons of transition, healing and self-discovery. She is also the founder of Creators Haven Global.

In session, you can expect a safe, respectful and non-judgemental space where you can be honest, feel heard, and take the steps toward healing and growth at your own pace.

Our approach

Insight, with tools you can actually use.

We combine professional psychological insight with emotional depth and practical, proven tools, to help you move from confusion to clarity, and from clarity to purposeful living.

Trauma-informed

We pay attention to what has happened to you, not only what’s happening now, and we go at a pace that feels safe.

CBT-based

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy helps you notice the thoughts and patterns that keep you stuck, and practise new ones.

Faith-sensitive

Where you ask for it and where it’s appropriate, your faith can be part of the conversation. It’s never imposed.

Before we begin

How sessions work.

Format and length

Sessions are typically 60 minutes. They’re held online by Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp video, whichever is most comfortable for you. Michelle always makes sure her end is private and safe. In-person sessions are available on request.

Some things take more than one session. When that happens, we agree on a plan that supports your healing, whether that means booking more sessions or shaping a plan around your life.

Rescheduling

Please let us know at least 24 hours before your session if you need to reschedule. Life happens. Late cancellations (under 24 hours) attract a 50% fee, and a missed session without notice counts as used.

Confidentiality

Everything you share stays private, except where there’s a risk of harm to you or others, where the law requires disclosure, or where you give written consent.

If your needs would be better met by another professional, Michelle will tell you and refer you appropriately. Counselling is only offered within her area of training and expertise.
